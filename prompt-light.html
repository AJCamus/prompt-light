Prompt Light — full-screen teleprompter

Paste your script below (or open a .txt / .md file). Multi-page documents just flow one after another — page breaks are ignored. Mark sections with a tag line like [VERSE 1], [CHORUS], or a ## Heading — the prompter pauses when a tag reaches the red guide line, so you can take your break, then hit Space to roll on. The screen is full white on purpose: it doubles as your camera light.
Keys while prompting: Space play / pause / resume  •  ↑ ↓ speed  •  ← → nudge  •  R restart  •  F full screen  •  Esc back to edit
